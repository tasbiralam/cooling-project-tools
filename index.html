Formulas & method
$$\omega = \dfrac{2\pi \cdot RPM}{60}$$

ω = 157.08 rad/s

$$v_{tip} = \omega \cdot r$$

vtip = 9.11 m/s  (r = 58 mm)

$$Q \approx A \cdot v_{tip} \cdot \eta$$

Q ≈ 0.04814 m³/s  (A = 0.01057 m², η = 0.50)

$$Re = \dfrac{\rho \cdot v_{tip} \cdot r}{\mu}$$

Re = 35,763

Physical interpretation

Velocity field
Tip speed increases linearly with radius, so the highest velocities appear at the blade tips. Near the hub and outside the rotor plane, velocity approaches zero.

Pressure field
Each blade has a pressure face (driving flow) and a suction face. The pressure difference is the aerodynamic origin of the delivered airflow, analogous to lift on an aerofoil.

Inlet and outlet boundaries were set to fixed atmospheric pressure (0 Pa). Domain-average pressure is not a meaningful metric; the local pressure difference across the blades is the relevant quantity.

Workflow

Design → Predict → Verify
  • Design — 5-blade axial fan modelled in CAD; geometry extracted for analysis.
  • Predict — Airflow estimated from tip speed, swept area and efficiency factor.
  • Verify — Steady RANS in COMSOL (k-ε, Frozen Rotor) on the actual CAD geometry.
